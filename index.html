1. Introduction

Mark Day (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an attendance and leave management application developed and operated by Athratech (“Athratech,” “the Company”). This Privacy Policy explains what information Mark Day collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices and rights available to users in connection with the App’s attendance, leave management, location-based attendance, and facial verification features.

This Policy applies to individuals who use the App as employees of an organization that has deployed Mark Day (each, an “Employer”), and to administrators who manage the App on an Employer’s behalf.

2

2. Employer as Data Controller; Athratech’s Role

Mark Day is provided to organizations for use by their employees. As between Athratech and the Employer, the Employer determines the purposes and means of processing personal data collected through the App (including which employees use it, what attendance and leave rules apply, and how long records are needed for the Employer’s internal purposes), and acts as the data controller / data fiduciary for that data. Athratech acts as a data processor on the Employer’s behalf, processing personal data solely to provide, maintain, and support the App in accordance with the Employer’s instructions and this Policy.

Employees should also refer to their Employer’s internal HR and data protection policies, as the Employer is responsible for how attendance and leave data is used within the organization (e.g., for payroll, performance, or disciplinary purposes). Athratech does not control, and is not responsible for, an Employer’s internal use of data obtained through the App.

3

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

Employee ID and password (or other credentials) used for authentication and access to the App.

3.2 Attendance Information

Check-in time, check-out time, attendance date, work duration, and attendance status (e.g., Present, Absent, Late).

3.3 Leave Information

Leave type, leave dates, leave reason, leave request status, leave balance, and leave history.

3.4 Location Information

Where an Employer has enabled location-based attendance, the App collects device location information at the time of check-in/check-out to verify that attendance is being marked from an approved location.

Location is collected only when the relevant attendance action is performed and is not tracked continuously in the background.

3.5 Camera and Facial Verification Information

Where an Employer has enabled facial verification, the App uses the device camera to capture a facial image at the time of check-in/check-out for identity verification.

[Company to confirm and state precisely: whether facial images/templates are processed on-device and immediately discarded, or transmitted to and stored on Athratech’s or a third party’s servers, and for how long.]

3.6 Device and Technical Information

The App may automatically collect limited technical information necessary for the App to function, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash/diagnostic logs.

4. Consent and Legal Basis

By creating an account or using Mark Day, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Policy, to the extent required by applicable law.

Where facial verification or location tracking is used, the App will request the relevant device permission before first use, and the feature will only operate if that permission is granted.

You may withdraw consent by disabling the relevant permission in your device settings or by contacting your Employer’s administrator, though this may limit or disable certain App features.

Where required by applicable data protection law, processing of personal data, and in particular biometric data, is carried out on the basis of consent obtained from the employee, or another applicable legal basis identified by the Employer as data controller.

5. How Information Is Used

  • Employee authentication and account access
  • Check-in and check-out processing
  • Work duration and attendance status tracking
  • Monthly attendance calendar and reporting
  • Leave application, approval workflow, and leave history
  • Leave balance calculation
  • Attendance and identity verification
  • Attendance-related reminders and notifications
  • Maintaining the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the App
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
Important: Information is not used for advertising, and is not sold to third parties.

9. Data Security

Athratech implements reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures include access controls, encryption of data in transit, and restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Athratech cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and for reporting any suspected unauthorized access to their Employer or Athratech promptly.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and to the Employer’s role as data controller, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to the Employer’s legitimate record-keeping requirements and applicable law.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features such as facial verification, where applicable.
  • Raise a grievance regarding the handling of your personal data.

Requests should first be directed to your Employer’s administrator, who may coordinate with Athratech as needed.
